# Replace With Your Materials

Put real scanned materials, AI-generated textures, or Unity material assets here.

Recommended slots:

- `Road_Or_Sidewalk_Ground`: assign to `Ground Material`.
- `Excavated_Soil`: assign to `Soil Material`.
- `Dark_Pit_Floor`: assign to `Dark Pit Material`.
- `Debris_Rubble`: assign to `Debris Material`.
- `Broken_Ground_Edge`: assign to `Broken Edge Material` if you do not want to reuse `Ground Material`.

Leave a slot empty to use the plugin's automatic simple material.
