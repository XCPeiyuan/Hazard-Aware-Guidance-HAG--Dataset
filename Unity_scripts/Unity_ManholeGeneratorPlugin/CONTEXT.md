# Procedural Manhole Dataset Generation

This context covers a Unity plugin that generates simulated manhole scenes for dataset collection, especially scenes involving open manhole hazards relevant to visually impaired pedestrians.

## Language

**Procedural Manhole Assembly**:
A configurable Unity-generated manhole object composed of a shaft, raised rim, pipes, and an optional separate cover.
_Avoid_: Decorative manhole, simple cylinder, prop

**Open Manhole Hazard**:
A scene state where the manhole opening is exposed because the cover is absent, displaced, or otherwise not protecting the opening.
_Avoid_: Removed lid, uncovered hole, missing cap

**Cover Absent State**:
An **Open Manhole Hazard** where no **Cover** is visible near the opening.
_Avoid_: Missing lid

**Cover Displaced State**:
An **Open Manhole Hazard** where the **Cover** is visible away from the opening.
_Avoid_: Lid beside hole

**Cover Partial State**:
An **Open Manhole Hazard** where the **Cover** partially overlaps the opening but still leaves a dangerous exposed gap.
_Avoid_: Half-open lid

**Partial Open Amount**:
A normalized value from 0 to 1 describing how far the **Cover** has slid from fully covering the opening toward being fully displaced.
_Avoid_: Half amount, visible ratio, occlusion ratio

**Cover State**:
The mutually exclusive placement relationship between the **Cover** and the manhole opening: absent, displaced, or partial.
_Avoid_: Cover mode, hazard type

**Cover Damage**:
The physical damage condition of the **Cover**, independent from its placement relationship to the opening.
_Avoid_: Broken state, hazard state

**Cover**:
The separable physical object that normally closes or partially closes the manhole opening.
_Avoid_: Lid, cap

**Raised Rim**:
The narrow protruding ring or frame immediately around the manhole opening that remains visible when the cover is absent.
_Avoid_: Border, lip, trim

**Rim Cross-Section**:
The visible vertical profile of the **Raised Rim**, expected to have an outer trapezoid and an inner stepped trapezoid that forms a cover seat.
_Avoid_: Rounded ring, soft donut edge

**Cover Seat**:
The inner ledge in the **Raised Rim** that supports and locates the **Cover**.
_Avoid_: Floating cover, decorative groove

**Hazard Scenario**:
A configured dataset-generation case that places a **Procedural Manhole Assembly** into one **Open Manhole Hazard** state.
_Avoid_: Randomizer, sample, scene preset

**Annotation Workflow**:
The external process used to label generated images or scenes after the plugin has created them.
_Avoid_: Built-in bbox export

**Scene Randomization**:
The external process that varies camera, lighting, weather, ground surface, and background context around a generated hazard.
_Avoid_: Hazard scenario, manhole generator settings

**Construction Pit Hazard**:
A simulated road or sidewalk excavation hazard with an irregular opening, variable depth, and unstable-looking edge geometry.
_Avoid_: Simple hole, crater, trench

**Pit Footprint Mode**:
The high-level construction pit opening family, either blob-like or trench-like, before seed-based irregularity is applied.
_Avoid_: Separate hazard type

**Wall Roughness**:
Seeded small-scale surface variation on a sloped construction pit wall without adding separate stepped wall layers.
_Avoid_: Wall detail mode, soil strata

**Broken Edge**:
Thin, low-profile broken ground pieces placed around a **Construction Pit Hazard** opening.
_Avoid_: Barrier, curb, thick slab

**Generation Seed**:
A numeric value used to generate a reproducible randomized hazard shape within configured parameter ranges.
_Avoid_: Parameter code, full configuration string

**Ground Cutout Surface**:
A flat generated ground patch with a real opening around the manhole, aligned with the rim base so the shaft can meet the raised rim without a visible gap.
_Avoid_: Transparent ground, shader hole

**Auto Material**:
A simple procedural material generated by the plugin when no explicit material override is assigned.
_Avoid_: Final material, real material library

**Reflective Cover Material**:
A dark cast-iron cover material with enough metallic and smoothness response to show highlights when the Unity scene provides reflection data.
_Avoid_: Black flat cover

**Material Override Folder**:
The visible plugin folder reserved for user-provided scanned, authored, or AI-generated materials.
_Avoid_: Generated material cache

**Generated Surface Texture Mapping**:
The UV coordinate mapping applied to generated hazard geometry so user-assigned material textures render as visible surface detail instead of only changing base color.
_Avoid_: Material color, shader fix

**Ground Patch Texture Mapping**:
The UV mapping rule for generated ground patches: the entire local ground patch is treated as one 0-1 texture surface, even when the mesh contains a circular, rectangular, or irregular opening.
_Avoid_: Per-section ground tiling, repeated square patches

## Relationships

- A **Procedural Manhole Assembly** may present an **Open Manhole Hazard**
- A **Hazard Scenario** controls one **Procedural Manhole Assembly**
- A **Cover** belongs to one **Procedural Manhole Assembly** but can be separated from its opening
- A **Raised Rim** surrounds exactly one manhole opening
- A **Rim Cross-Section** should preserve hard edges so the **Raised Rim** looks like a manufactured frame
- A **Cover Seat** belongs to the **Raised Rim** and gives the **Cover** a believable resting position
- An **Open Manhole Hazard** can be represented as a **Cover Absent State**, **Cover Displaced State**, or **Cover Partial State**
- A **Cover State** is mutually exclusive; **Cover Damage** is a separate future extension
- A **Cover Partial State** is controlled by **Partial Open Amount** and an opening direction, not by direct bounding-box or area annotations
- A **Ground Cutout Surface** belongs to a **Procedural Manhole Assembly** when a standalone embedded-ground preview is needed
- The **Annotation Workflow** is outside the plugin and does not define how the **Hazard Scenario** is generated
- **Scene Randomization** is outside the first plugin version; a **Hazard Scenario** only controls the manhole assembly and cover state
- A **Construction Pit Hazard** is generated from a **Generation Seed** plus visible parameter ranges, not from a manually decoded parameter string
- A first-version **Construction Pit Hazard** includes only the exposed opening, pit walls, pit floor, local ground, and nearby debris
- A **Pit Footprint Mode** changes the shape family of a **Construction Pit Hazard** without changing the hazard scope
- **Wall Roughness** makes sloped pit walls uneven while preserving a single-slope wall model
- A **Broken Edge** belongs to the local ground around a **Construction Pit Hazard** and defaults to the ground material
- An **Auto Material** is used only when a matching user material is not assigned
- A **Reflective Cover Material** still depends on scene lighting, skybox, or reflection probes to show visible reflections
- A **Material Override Folder** provides a stable handoff location for future higher-quality materials
- **Generated Surface Texture Mapping** is required for custom generated meshes; without it, material overrides may change color while texture maps appear absent
- **Ground Patch Texture Mapping** applies to generated ground cutout surfaces for both manhole and construction pit hazards

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "When generating an **Open Manhole Hazard**, should the **Cover** be deleted?"
> **Domain expert:** "No, it may be absent or displaced; the dataset needs both cases because a nearby **Cover** can be part of the visual evidence."

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Realistic manhole" was resolved as simulation-oriented realism for visual dataset collection, not construction-grade CAD accuracy.
- "Open manhole cover" was corrected to **Open Manhole Hazard**; the detection target is the exposed hazardous opening, not the **Cover** object.
- Bounding boxes and label export are out of scope for the plugin because the **Annotation Workflow** happens in external tools.
- Camera, lighting, weather, ground, and background variation were resolved as **Scene Randomization**, not part of the first **Hazard Scenario** scope.
- **Cover State** describes placement only; future broken or cracked covers belong to **Cover Damage**, not to the placement state.
- "Half-covered" was resolved as **Partial Open Amount**, a slide amount from fully covered toward displaced, rather than a directly measured exposed area ratio.
- Generated concrete, cover, dark opening, and pipe textures were resolved as **Auto Material** fallbacks, not replacements for future scanned or AI-generated materials.
- The **Raised Rim** should be a narrow frame around the opening, not a thick top slab covering the full shaft wall.
- Ground intersection was resolved as a **Ground Cutout Surface** with actual geometry, not a transparent shader or masking workflow.
- The **Ground Cutout Surface** should align with the **Raised Rim** base while the shaft side walls reach that same base height.
- The **Ground Cutout Surface** should be visually flat; clearance below it may exist, but it should not render as a raised slab or visible border.
- The **Ground Cutout Surface** opening should follow the manhole opening size, not the outside of the **Raised Rim**.
- The **Rim Cross-Section** was refined to use an outer trapezoid and an inner stepped profile with a **Cover Seat**.
- The shaft side walls should reach the bottom of the **Raised Rim**; avoid a visible gap between shaft and rim.
- The **Generation Seed** controls reproducible variation; explicit Inspector ranges control the allowed shape family.
- Construction context objects such as cones, barriers, warning signs, workers, and machinery are outside the first **Construction Pit Hazard** scope.
- Long narrow excavations are represented by **Pit Footprint Mode** `Trench`; block-like excavations are represented by `Blob`.
- Pit walls remain sloped in the first version; roughness is applied as surface variation, not as distinct benches or soil layers.
- **Broken Edge** is a ground-surface detail, not construction context and not a thick exposed cross-section.
- Material overrides require **Generated Surface Texture Mapping** on custom meshes; Unity primitives may show textures already because they include built-in UVs.
- Generated ground should use **Ground Patch Texture Mapping**, not per-section texture repetition, because the local patch represents one continuous piece of sidewalk or road.
